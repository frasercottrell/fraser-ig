# Trial reel hooks, batch 15 (2026-09-16) + talking head ideas

First batch written by Runneth. Built on the call-3 rule (not watching has to cost something), the three hook
families, "ads" in nearly every line, brands contextualised. Checked against the 92 hooks already in `fraser-ig/posts/`
for duplicates. One line on screen, no tails, tight first frame (camera-rig POV or unusual BTS), trending audio.
Keyword only where a real delivery exists.

## Trial reel hooks (12), v2: every hook is a variation on a logged winner

Fraser, 2026-09-16: "the trial reels you need to build from whats working in the past." v1 was too invented. Each
hook below names its parent reel and the number that earned it a place. Frame = the parent's frame family unless
stated (camera-rig POV with an ad on the monitor, or the unusual BTS shot; both ran 28 to 35% skip).

Test variable: family angle only. Same frame family, same audio approach, same caption standard, so the hook is
the one thing that moved.

## Talking head ideas (6, all cut for under 25s)

Each: opening line (spoken + on screen together, since the 5-hooks reel matched them), the beat, the keyword.

1. **"5 hooks" sequel: 5 ad openers that are dead.** Spoken/on-screen: "These 5 ad hooks used to print money. Now they get skipped." List the five on screen one at a time (product shot open, "introducing", founder wave, discount first, "are you tired of"). Close: "Comment HOOKS for the 34 that still work." Same shape as the 7k reel, inverted.
2. **The 48-hour rule.** "Every ad I launch has 48 hours to live." Beat: the brands that scale kill faster than they launch; most founders keep a loser running for a fortnight out of loyalty; the budget that loser ate was the next winner's test. Close: FORMAT. (Replaced the Ryze idea 2026-09-16, already filmed by Fraser.)
3. **The 3 static formats, cut to 20s.** The 40s version did 806 views. Re-cut: one line each, the ads on screen, no explanation. "Three statics carry whole accounts. Native story. Headline. Review. That's it." STATIC.
4. **Sep 15 re-do.** "Winning brands don't have better ads. They have more dead ones." Beat: the winners you see are the survivors of 40 flops you didn't. Cost: if you're launching 4 a month you'll never find one. FORMAT or soft.
5. **Your review is the ad.** "Stop writing ads. Your customer already wrote a better one." Show a review, drag it onto a product shot, done, in one take. STATIC.
6. **The fake podcast, operator cut.** "Your competitor's podcast isn't a podcast. It's 40 ads wearing a jumper." Beat: one 20-minute fake conversation, cut into every angle, none of them look like ads. Close: YAP.
