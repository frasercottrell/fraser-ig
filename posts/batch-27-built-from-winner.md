**Hook:** "I've made 10,000 ads. These 3 first lines outsell everything else."

## 3. Subject: AD TYPES
**Hook:** "I've made 10,000 ads. These 3 ad types sell in any niche."
**Caption:**
Skincare, supplements, SaaS, doesn't matter. Three formats always convert cold:
1. Problem, agitate, solve.
2. Founder to camera, telling the story.
3. Us vs them comparison.
Simple, repeatable, they work everywhere.

## 4. Subject: EDITS / retention
**Hook:** "I've made 10,000 ads. These 3 edits keep people watching to the end."
**Caption:**
Retention is where ads live or die. Three edits fix it:
1. Cut the first second. Start on the point.
2. A visual change every 2 to 3 seconds.
3. Captions that reveal word by word.
Same footage, double the watch time.
Comment 'HOOKS' for the openings that hold attention.

Sometimes it isn't the ad, it's the offer. These 3 always pull:
1. Risk reversal (money back, try free).
2. Bonus stack (same price, more value).
3. Real urgency (not a fake timer).
Fix the offer and average creative starts winning.
