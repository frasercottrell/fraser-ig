# Batch 22. EXPLORE batch - 10 wildly DIFFERENT hooks (not variants of one template)

## Why this batch
The last rounds were all near-duplicates: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. These 5 hooks did [X]." Similar hooks give
similar results - you can only nudge the current winner, never find a new one. To find NEW winners you have to
test WIDE. Every hook below is a different beast: different structure, emotion, subject, length. Deliberately
almost nothing in common with each other.

## Exploit vs explore
- **Batch 21 = exploit** (squeeze the proven "5 hooks" winner with small result-driven tweaks).
- **Batch 22 = explore** (find the next winner in a totally different part of the map).
Run explore batches to discover; once something new pops, exploit it with a rotation like batch 21.

## Rules held
Winning tight POV footage (or vary the tight frame), SHORT caption per hook, trending audio (↑), HOOKS CTA
(soft where the topic doesn't fit). ⚠️ Any specific figure must be true / standable (flagged inline).

## The 10 hooks - each a different structure/emotion/subject

### 1. QUESTION / fear
**Hook:** "Why do most ads die in the first 3 seconds?"
**Caption:** Most ads are dead before the 3-second mark. Not the offer, not the edit. The opening. They lead with the product instead of the problem, so the viewer never sees themselves in it and scrolls. Open on their problem, in their words, and the drop-off disappears. Comment 'HOOKS' for openings that actually stop people.

### 2. BOLD CONTRARIAN CLAIM
**Hook:** "Pretty ads don't sell anymore."
**Caption:** My best-performing ad this month looked terrible. Phone footage, no edit, bad lighting. It beat every polished ad we made. Polish reads as "advert" and gets skipped. Rough reads as content and gets watched. Stop making them pretty. Make them real. Comment 'HOOKS' for the openings I use on the ugly ones that win.

### 3. CONFESSION / story with stakes (⚠️ verify the figure)
**Hook:** "One line cost my client £40k." (verify - or use "thousands")
**Caption:** Great product, big budget, barely any sales for a month. We changed one thing: the first line, from the product to the customer's problem. Same everything else. It turned around overnight. The whole ad was one line away from working. Most are. Comment 'HOOKS' and I'll send you the openings that fix it.

### 4. CALLOUT / identity
**Hook:** "Founders: stop approving your own ads."
**Caption:** You're too close to your product to judge your ads. You approve what makes you proud, not what stops a stranger. The ugly one you almost killed is usually the winner. The polished one you love usually flops. Judge on the data, not your taste. Comment 'HOOKS' for openings that actually stop people.

### 5. MISTAKE / curiosity story
**Hook:** "The best ad I ever ran was a mistake."
**Caption:** We uploaded the wrong cut by accident. Rough, unfinished, no polish. It beat the "final" version by miles. The thing we'd fixed in the polished one was the exact thing that made it feel human. Stop over-producing. Ship the rough one. Comment 'HOOKS' for openings that make ads feel real.

### 6. AUTHORITY + new subject (NOT "5 hooks")
**Hook:** "I've made 10,000 ads. Here's the only metric I actually trust."
**Caption:** Everyone stares at ROAS. I barely look at it first. The first thing I check is hook rate: what percent get past 3 seconds. If that's low, nothing downstream matters. A great offer can't save an ad nobody watches. Fix the stop before anything else. Comment 'HOOKS' for the openings that lift it.

### 7. FOMO / competitive intrigue
**Hook:** "Your competitor knows your customer better than you do."
**Caption:** Not because they're smarter. Because they test more. Every ad they ship is a question to the market. 30 ads a month is 30 answers about what your customer wants. You run 3 and guess. Out-test them and you'll out-know them. Comment 'HOOKS' to start testing faster.

### 8. COMMAND / steal-the-formula
**Hook:** "Steal the ad formula I use for every client."
**Caption:** Problem first. Then proof it's for them. Then hold the answer back. Then, only then, the product. That's the order under almost every winner I've made. Most brands do the opposite: product first, problem never. That's why they get skipped. Comment 'HOOKS' for the openings that nail step one.

### 9. PROVOCATIVE STAT / fear
**Hook:** "You're wasting most of your ad budget."
**Caption:** Not on targeting. On ads nobody watches past 3 seconds. If your hook rate is under 30%, you're paying to show a dead ad to more people, and more budget just burns faster. The fix isn't spend. It's a first line that stops the right person. Comment 'HOOKS' for the ones that do.

### 10. VIRAL-PATTERN / curiosity
**Hook:** "Every ad that's gone viral for me had one thing in common."
**Caption:** Not the budget, the actor, or the edit. Every single one opened on the viewer's problem, not the product. First line, every time. That's the whole pattern. Everything else is decoration. Comment 'HOOKS' and I'll send you the openings that do it.

## Read-out
Run these across the tight POV footage, track views + saves + comments + follows. The point is RANGE - find
which totally-different angle beats the "5 hooks" winner (5,948). Whatever pops, we then exploit with a rotation.
