# Batch 24. THE ZONE - same winning skeleton, genuinely different promise each.

## Calibration (so we stop swinging between too-similar and too-different)
- TOO SIMILAR = synonym swaps on the same claim (batch 23: sales -> revenue, 5 -> 7). Low learning.
- THE ZONE (this batch) = keep the proven skeleton, change the IDEA/PROMISE. Same bones, different substance.
- TOO DIFFERENT = new structure/format entirely (batch 22). Scattered.

## The proven skeleton (keep)
"I've [analysed/made] 10,000 ads. These 5 [noun] [distinct promise]."
Authority number + the "5" + a reveal + a concrete promise. Control to beat: reel 041, 5,948 views.
Same winning footage + short caption (caption already generic enough to sit under all of these).

## Variants - each a DIFFERENT promise, same structure

1. UNIVERSALITY: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. These 5 hooks work in every niche."
2. AVOID/MISTAKES: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. The best ones all avoided these 5 mistakes."
3. EXCLUSIVITY: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. Only 5 hook types actually convert."
4. BEATS BUDGET: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. These 5 hooks beat a bigger budget every time."
5. DESERT-ISLAND: "I've made 10,000 ads. If I could keep only 5 hooks, it'd be these."
6. CONVERSION: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. These 5 hooks turn strangers into buyers."
7. NEVER FLOP: "I've made 10,000 ads. These 5 formats have never flopped."
8. TIMELESS: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. These 5 hooks have worked for a decade and still do."
9. INSIDER SECRET: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. The 5 hooks winning brands quietly reuse."
10. EASE: "I've analysed 10,000 ads. These 5 hooks basically write themselves."

## Read-out
Same footage + caption, rotate the hook. Because the STRUCTURE is constant but the PROMISE differs, whichever
beats 5,948 tells us which promise the audience wants most (universality vs mistakes vs exclusivity vs budget
vs conversion...). That's a real learning, not a synonym result. Then exploit the winning promise with tighter
tweaks.
