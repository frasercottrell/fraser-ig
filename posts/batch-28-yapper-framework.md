## What a yapper is (so the content stays accurate)
A relatable hero (The Convert) who went through something, found the product, and talks to camera telling the
story - like FaceTiming a friend. Fully scripted, story-first, DR bones underneath. Competes with organic, not
other ads. NOT a rambling car clip, NOT unscripted, NOT a product-intro UGC clip.
