## 1. Pillar 1: Why Ads Work
*Source: "10,000+ ads made" proof · Style: authority + contrarian*

**On-screen hook:** "I've made over 10,000 ads. The winners all ignored the thing brands obsess over."

**Caption:**
10,000+ ads. You'd think the winners had the best production, the slickest edit, the biggest budget.

They didn't.

The ones that printed money were often the ugliest in the account. What they had was a first line that named the customer's problem before they could scroll. That's it. Brands pour weeks into the polish and minutes into the message, then wonder why the beautiful ad flops.

Message first. Pretty second. In that order or you're just decorating.

## 8. Pillar 4: Founder Perspective
*Source: hooks doc "founders" · Style: identity → pain (mirror, uncomfortable truth)*

**On-screen hook:** "You're the least qualified person to judge your own ads."

**Caption:**
You know too much. You know the founder story, the material sourcing, the reason the logo's that colour. Your customer knows none of it and cares about none of it.

So when you kill the "cringe" ad that's actually converting because it doesn't match how you see the brand, you're not protecting the business. You're taxing it with your own taste.

Judge the ad by the number, not by whether it flatters your idea of the brand.
