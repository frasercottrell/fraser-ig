## Reel 6.1 - F2 Competitive/accountability - CTA-D (share driver)
**On-screen hook:** "Your agency should show you this every week. Most hide it."
**B-roll:** tight/busy workbench or desk, hands on a laptop showing a report / dashboard. Arresting first frame, fill it.

**Caption:**

Save this before your next agency call 👇

If you pay someone to run your ads and you still can't answer "what worked this month," the reporting is broken on purpose. Here are the 5 things a good partner shows you every week, without being asked:

1. Hook rate per ad. The percent getting past 3 seconds, ad by ad. Not just spend and ROAS. If they can't tell you which hooks are stopping people, they don't know why anything worked, they're just watching a number move.

2. What they're testing, and why. A real plan names the variable: "problem-first hooks vs product-first this week." If the answer is a vague "we're optimising," there is no test, just spend disappearing.

3. Why the winner won. Anyone can point at the best ad. A good partner tells you the reason, the hook, the angle, the frame, so it can be repeated. If they can't explain it, they can't reproduce it, and neither can you.

4. What's queued next. Winners fatigue. If there's nothing in the pipeline behind the current best ad, you're one fade away from a cliff. You should always see what's coming.

5. What they killed, and why. A report that only shows wins is hiding the losses. The honest ones show you what got cut this week. That's proof they're managing the account, not babysitting it.

If you're not seeing these five, you're paying for activity, not results.

Know a founder whose agency does exactly this? Send them this 👇 it might save them a fortune.

## Reel 6.2 - F1 Contrarian - CTA-E (debate prompt)
**On-screen hook:** "Your creative matters more than your targeting. Fight me."
**B-roll:** tight/busy workbench, hands grabbing gear / a bold on-screen frame. Arresting first frame.

**Caption:**

Save this before you blame the algorithm again 👇

Everyone obsesses over audiences and budgets. I'll say the unpopular thing: in 2026, your creative is your targeting. Here's why the ad itself matters more than any setting:

1. The algorithm finds your buyer now. Broad targeting plus a sharp ad beats narrow targeting plus a dull one. Meta's machine does the finding, if you give it a clear signal to follow. That signal is the creative.

2. A strong hook tells the algorithm who it's for. When the right people stop, Meta learns fast and shows it to more of them. A weak hook gives it nothing to optimise toward. The creative trains the delivery.

3. Targeting can't fix a bad message. If the perfect person sees a flat ad, they still scroll. No audience setting rescues a line that doesn't land. The message does the persuading, not the placement.

4. Creative is where the leverage is. You can tweak audiences all day for marginal gains. A better hook can 5x your results overnight. Same spend, same product, different first line. That's the lever.

5. The best media buyers I know are creative-obsessed. They stopped fiddling with settings years ago. They pour their energy into more angles, sharper hooks, better first frames. That's where the wins actually come from.

Targeting is table stakes. Creative is the game.

## Reel 6.3 - F3 Pattern/authority - CTA-F (series follow-hook)
**On-screen hook:** "I audited 100 ad accounts this year. The same 5 mistakes every time."
**B-roll:** tight/busy workbench, hands scrolling a dashboard / stacked account screenshots. Arresting first frame.

**Caption:**

Save this and check your account against it 👇

I went through 100 ad accounts this year. Different niches, different budgets, the same 5 mistakes on repeat. If your ads have stalled, you're probably making at least two of these:

1. Everything opens on the product. Ad after ad leading with the brand, the feature, the launch. Not one opening on the customer's problem. The fix is free and it's the single biggest lever: open on them, not you.

2. Ten ads, one idea. What looked like a test was the same concept in ten outfits. That's not testing, it's repeating. Real testing means genuinely different angles, problems, and hooks competing against each other.

3. Scaling winners too fast. An ad pops, they double the budget overnight, the delivery resets and it dies. Winners need to grow into spend slowly, not get shoved into it. Patience is a performance lever.

4. No pipeline. One winner carrying the whole account, nothing queued behind it. When it fatigues, and it will, the account falls off a cliff. Healthy accounts always have the next ad ready.

5. Watching ROAS only. ROAS tells you what happened, not whether it lasts. They miss the leading signals, hook rate, hold rate, frequency, until the drop is already here. Read those first.

Fix even two of these and most accounts turn around within weeks.

This is 1 of 5 account breakdowns I'm posting this week. Follow @frasercottrell so the next 4 find you 👇
