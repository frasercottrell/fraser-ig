1. 6 ways to film a yapper ad so it doesn't look like everyone else's
2. most of the yapper ads you're seeing right now are probably someone sat in their car
3. which is fine... until every brand in your niche is filming the exact same shot
4. 1. walk and talk / phone in hand, walking somewhere. the movement in the first frame does a lot of the stopping for you
5. 2. getting ready in the mirror / doing their hair or skincare, talking the whole time. feels like a story they'd post anyway
6. 3. mid-task / making a coffee, packing a bag, cooking. hands are busy so the product can just sit in shot
7. 4. the handheld mic / talking straight into one of those little handheld mics. looks like they're making a video for their own page
8. 5. green screen / stand in front of a Reddit thread or a review and react to it. the proof is sat right there behind them
9. 6. lying in bed / phone held above their face, late night chat energy. low-fi on purpose
10. If you want the script to go with it, i made you my yapper framework, comment YAP
