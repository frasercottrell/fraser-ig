## AI-1. "AI is bad at the one thing everyone uses it for" (Pillar 2, contrarian framework)

**On-screen hook:** "Everyone's using AI to write their ads. It's the one thing it's worst at."

**Caption:**

Save this. Where AI actually helps in ad creative, and the one place it quietly ruins it 👇

AI is brilliant at the grunt work and terrible at the part that decides whether the ad works. Get it backwards and you'll ship a hundred polished ads that all sound the same.

Where it earns its keep:

1. Research. Mining reviews, pulling angles, spotting patterns in your data. Fast.
2. Variations. Ten cuts, ten formats, ten thumbnails from one idea.
3. The repetitive stuff. Resizing, localising, rough edits.

Where it kills you:

4. The hook and the script. AI writes the average of every ad ever made, and average doesn't stop a thumb. The words a human reads have to come from a human. Or a real customer.

Use AI for the reps. Keep the thinking.

## AI-3. "AI tools worth it vs hype" (Pillar 2, framework) · soft CTA

**On-screen hook:** "You're paying for AI tools that make your ads worse."

**Caption:**

Save this before your next AI tool subscription. How to tell the ones worth it from the hype 👇

Every week there's a new AI tool promising to fix your ads. Most make them worse, just faster. Here's how we decide what's actually worth using:

1. Does it save real time on the boring stuff? Editing, resizing, variations. Worth it.
2. Does it try to do the thinking for you? Auto-writing your hooks, auto-picking your angle. Bin it. That's the part that has to be human.
3. Does it make the ad look more like AI, or more like a human made it? If people can tell a machine made it, it's costing you trust.

The tools that win give you leverage on the grunt work. The ones that lose try to replace the judgment.
