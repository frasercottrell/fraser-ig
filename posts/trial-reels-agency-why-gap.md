# Trial Reels. Focused Angle: "Your agency can't explain WHY the ad worked"

One angle, many hooks. Core truth carried across all of them:
**a winning ad you can't explain is luck, and luck doesn't scale. A real partner tells you the
mechanism. This hook, this angle, why it landed. And builds the next win on it. If wins are
surprises and losses are "the algorithm," you're paying for guessing.**

This is a hook-test battery: post these against each other, keep the one that pulls, kill the rest.
Each tagged with the **hook style** being tested.

Format: text hook on screen + 5–7s B-roll + trending audio + caption + 2 CTAs.

**Freebie fit:** this angle converts people who already have an agency. A tailored lead magnet beats a
generic one here. Suggest **"The 5 questions that reveal whether your agency actually knows what's
working"** → keyword **WHY**. (Freebie to build; using WHY below.)

## 1. Mirror / identity

**On-screen hook (one line):** "If your agency can't tell you why your best ad worked, they didn't make it work."

**Caption**. *first line is the driver; it's all that shows before "…more", so it does the work of pulling them in:*

There's one question that instantly tells you whether your agency knows what they're doing 👇

Point at your best-performing ad and ask them: "why did this one work?"

A real partner answers with the mechanism. This hook, this angle, this exact pain it hit. And here's the next ad we build on it. A guessing one reaches for the algorithm, the audience, the season. Anything but the creative.

If they can't tell you why it worked, they can't repeat it. That's not a strategy you're paying for. It's a subscription to a slot machine.
