**Caption** — *first line is the driver (the "…more" preview):*

Save this before you write your next ad — the 3 jobs every scroll-stopping hook does in the first 3 seconds 👇

Most ads open by talking about the product. That's exactly why most ads get skipped.

The winners all do three things before the third second:

1. Name the exact person. Not "everyone" — the specific someone. "DTC founders spending £50k a month on Meta" beats "business owners" every time. The right person has to feel you're in the room with them.

2. Hit a problem they already feel. Not a feature. The tension that was in their head before your ad showed up. You're not creating the pain. You're naming it back to them.

3. Open a loop. Give them a reason the next three seconds are worth it — a claim, a question, a "here's why" they now need answered.

Identity. A real tension. A loop they need closed. Miss one and the scroll wins.
