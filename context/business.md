# Business Context — Offers, Funnel, Assets

## The two offers

**1. Fraggell Productions (the agency) — high ticket, done-for-you**
- Retainers start at **~$10k/month minimum**
- Clients need to be spending **~$80k+/month on paid media** — relatively large brands
- Buyer: **founders, heads of marketing, heads of growth**
- What it does: makes performing ad creative for **Meta and TikTok**

**2. The Ad Creative Course — low ticket, DIY (~$900)**
- Teaches **e-commerce brands how to make performing ad creative** for Meta & TikTok
- The funnel entry point: get people in cheap, some graduate into agency clients
- Newer launch — not yet featured properly in bio/links

**The funnel:** content → course ($900, DIY) → agency ($10k+/mo, DFY). Both served by the same authority-building content.

## Current lead-gen (what already works)

- **Twitter** — ~90% of the agency's growth. Organic, never run ads. ~80% male, US/UK.
- **YouTube** — weekly long-form. Strong on AI topics. (No Shorts.)
- **LinkedIn** — ghostwritten.
- Instagram is the **new channel** being added. Most personal brand output is outsourced; the point of this context is to run IG with a real plan.

## Email list / newsletters (owned audience)

- **Nice Ads** — every Monday, breakdown of **3 ads** from Fraser's feed. **~12,000 subs.** Runs paid ads for it; an acquisition source for the agency; drives traffic to Instagram. Lead magnet = a mini-course (large database of old content — *probably too overwhelming as a freebie*).
- **Second newsletter** — general signups, promo material.
- **Free UGC script framework** — video + doc download. ~100 signups/month from the website. Performs well because people *think* UGC scripting is their problem (it isn't, but the felt need is real). Simple to consume = good freebie model.

## Freebie strategy (from Georgia)

Keep freebies short (1–2 pages), one per content pillar, rotate them. Each freebie must relate to the content pushing it. Fraser's picks (best fit for his audience):
- ✅ **Ad creative swipe file** (20 high-performing ads + annotations on why they work)
- ✅ **30 high-converting ad hooks** (problem / curiosity / authority / story) — hook docs go crazy on IG
- ✅ **"Why your ads don't convert" PDF** (5 common mistakes + fixes) — curiosity + addresses a pain
- ❌ Audit checklist — skip, attracts pre-launch beginners (wrong journey stage)

Promote via **keyword CTAs + ManyChat**: "Comment AD", "Comment HOOKS", etc. (drives engagement signals + starts conversations).
