# Audience — Who the Instagram Is For

## Who they are

- E-commerce **brand owners / founders**
- **Heads of marketing, heads of growth**, senior marketing professionals
- Decision-makers or close to it — the people who buy ad creative or hire for it

## Demographics (from Fraser's existing channels)

- **~80% male**
- Split **US + UK**
- Age: **18–24, 25–34, 35–44** (those three bands)

## Pain points

- Low performance on their **Meta / TikTok ads** → **stagnant e-commerce growth**
- Internal team isn't hitting KPIs
- They **already know ad creative is the problem** — almost everyone who comes to Fraser knows this. The gap is:
  - **No time** to do it themselves
  - **No skill** to do it themselves
  - They want **more volume** (the volume game on Meta is getting bigger; they may already have an internal team + 1–2 agencies and still want more)

## What they want to hear about

- Why ads actually work (psychology, hooks, structure)
- How to make performing creative for Meta & TikTok
- Insider agency knowledge they can't get elsewhere
- Proof it works (real results)
- Practical things they can apply now

## Note

This audience is **further along** than beginners. Content aimed at "people who've never launched an ad" attracts the wrong crowd. Keep it pitched at people already running ads who want them to perform better / scale.
