# Results Log — Real Performance Data

The flywheel. Log every posted reel: hook, metrics, what the data says, what we change next.
Read results as data — double down on what works, cut what doesn't. One reel is noise; patterns are signal.

## What "good" looks like on a trial reel (reference)

- **Skip rate — lower is better.** This is the hook working (the 3-second stop). The #1 trial-reel signal.
- **Avg watch time** — the hold. On a 5–7s B-roll loop, ~5s+ is solid.
- **Follows** — the actual goal of trial reels. Converts at the *profile* as much as the reel.
- **Comments** — driven by the comment-keyword CTA; also the engagement that snowballs reach.
- **Saves** — only expect these on *saveable* (utility) content, not hot-take/opinion pieces.
- **Shares** — social currency; expect on novel/contrarian/teardown pieces.

Match the signal you chase to the *job* of the piece (see operating-model.md: saveable vs shareable).

## Reel 001 — Agency why-gap (mirror hook)

- **Posted:** ~30 Jul 2026 · data pulled ~19h later
- **On-screen hook:** "If your agency can't tell you why your best ad worked, they didn't make it work…"
- **B-roll:** studio, setting up the amaran lights (recognisable location ✅, one thing on screen ✅)
- **Caption:** Fraser's own version (why-gap → "the best marketers ask WHY" → soft follow CTA `@frasercottrell`). No comment-keyword / no freebie.

**Metrics (19h):** Views 1,225 · Reached 824 · Avg watch **6s** · Follows **0** · Likes 4 · Comments 0 · Shares ~1 · Saves 0
- Skip **35.1% (Lower / good)** · Share 0.1% (Higher / good) · Like 0.5% (Lower) · Save 0.0% (typical) · Repost 0.0% (typical)

**Read:**
- ✅ **Hook works.** Low skip + 6s watch = the scroll-stop and hold are landing. The hardest part is working.
- ✅ Share rate above typical — the angle carries some social currency.
- ❌ **Conversion leaked:** 0 follows / 0 comments / 0 saves from 824 reached. They watched, didn't act.

**Likely causes (most-likely first):**
1. **Profile not set up** — cold viewers tap through to an unclear bio/no highlights → no follow. Follows convert *at the profile*. (Stranger test still unaddressed = biggest suspect.)
2. **No comment-keyword CTA** — soft "drop a follow" instead of "comment WHY for [freebie]". Nothing manufacturing comments or capturing leads.
3. **Abstract payoff** — caption teaches a principle but leaves nothing concrete to save.
4. **n=1** — single reel, not a pattern.

**Actions:**
- [ ] Set up bio + 1–2 highlights **before** next posts (highest leverage, one-time)
- [ ] Add comment-keyword CTA + build the "5 questions" WHY freebie
- [ ] Test a variant with the 3 questions listed in-caption (gives a save-reason)
- [ ] Post the rest of the battery, compare skip / watch / follows

**Early hypothesis (hold loosely):** the *agency call-out / mirror* hook style stops the scroll well. Keep testing it. The blocker on results is downstream of the hook — profile + CTA — not the hook itself.

- **On-screen hook:** "I've studied thousands of winning ads. Their hooks all do the same 3 things…"

**Read — this is the important one:**
- **The saveable framework did exactly its job.** Reel 001 (hot take) got 0 saves; this framework got 3 saves + 1 follow + 2 comments. Different content job → different signal, as predicted in operating-model.md.

- **The first follow came from VALUE content, not a hot take.** Confirms Georgia: people follow once you give them a reason (utility), not just a stop.
- **The comment-keyword CTA works.** Adding it (the lever missing from 001) produced the first keyword comment. Keep it on every saveable reel.

**Read:**
- **Weakest hook of the four.** "Best copywriter is your customer, here's why…" is a clever *aphorism*, not a *pain*. It doesn't name a sharp tension the viewer feels — it's an abstract insight that requires you to already care about copywriting. Clever ≠ stopping.

- Was meant to be saveable, got 0 saves — **a saveable payoff is worthless if the hook doesn't stop the right person first.** Hook gates everything.

## 🔑 Hook stopping-power ranking (n=4 — this is the real finding)

Skip rate, best → worst (lower = better stop):

| Reel | Hook style | Skip | Watch | Converts? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 001 agency why-gap | **Identity mirror** ("your agency…") | **35.1% ✅** | 6s | reach, but 0 saves/follows |
| 002 pretty-before-clear | Personal story ("my biggest mistake…") | 40.3% ✅ | 4s | low |
| 003 3-jobs-of-a-hook | Authority framework ("I studied thousands…") | 48.6% | 5s | **1 follow, 3 saves, keyword lead** |
| 004 customer-copywriter | Abstract aphorism ("best copywriter is…") | 62.3% ❌ | 2s | nothing |

**The rule this gives us:** the more the hook names the **viewer** and a **sharp, felt tension**, the better it stops. Identity/pain hooks stop best; abstract/clever statements stop worst. This is Identity → Pain → Curiosity, proven on Fraser's own account.

**The two-lever model, confirmed:**
- **Hook** decides *how many* (and how qualified) stop. → lead with identity + pain, never an aphorism.
- **Content job** decides *what they do* after. → saveable frameworks convert (saves/follows/keyword leads); hot takes get reach; abstract insights get neither.
- **Winning combo = identity/pain hook + saveable framework payoff.** (Reel 003 had the payoff but a soft authority hook; 001 had the hook but a no-save hot take. Fuse them.)

**Secondary levers:** bright/dynamic B-roll > dark/static; trending audio (↑) matters; "…here's why" tails are weak — cut them.

## Patterns forming (n=6 — firming up)

1. **Two hook jobs, both valid — match to intent:**
   - *Identity/mirror hooks* ("your agency…", "you're paying…") → **max cold reach + stop** (Reel 001). Best for top-of-funnel awareness.
   - *Educational/authority hooks* ("I studied thousands… 3 things") → **higher skip, but saves + comments + follows** (Reel 003). Best for conversion + attracting the right people.
   - **Next experiment:** fuse them — an identity/"you" hook (high stop) in front of saveable framework content (high conversion). Best of both.
2. **Saveable framework content converts to follows/saves; hot takes get reach but 0 saves.** Make more frameworks — they're what grows the *right* audience.
3. **Personal-story hooks ("my biggest mistake") underperform cold** (Reel 002) — save them for the feed / post-follow.
4. **Comment-keyword CTA works — always include it on saveable reels** (and deliver the freebie!).
5. **Follows are coming now (1 so far)** — the profile is converting *some*. Still set it up properly to lift the rate.
6. **Check audio is trending (↑)** — likely behind Reel 002's low reach.
