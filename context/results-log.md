# Results Log — Real Performance Data

The flywheel. Log every posted reel: hook, metrics, what the data says, what we change next.
Read results as data — double down on what works, cut what doesn't. One reel is noise; patterns are signal.

## What "good" looks like on a trial reel (reference)

- **Skip rate — lower is better.** This is the hook working (the 3-second stop). The #1 trial-reel signal.
- **Avg watch time** — the hold. On a 5–7s B-roll loop, ~5s+ is solid.
- **Follows** — the actual goal of trial reels. Converts at the *profile* as much as the reel.
- **Comments** — driven by the comment-keyword CTA; also the engagement that snowballs reach.
- **Saves** — only expect these on *saveable* (utility) content, not hot-take/opinion pieces.
- **Shares** — social currency; expect on novel/contrarian/teardown pieces.

Match the signal you chase to the *job* of the piece (see operating-model.md: saveable vs shareable).

## Reel 001 — Agency why-gap (mirror hook)

- **Posted:** ~30 Jul 2026 · data pulled ~19h later
- **On-screen hook:** "If your agency can't tell you why your best ad worked, they didn't make it work…"
- **B-roll:** studio, setting up the amaran lights (recognisable location ✅, one thing on screen ✅)
- **Caption:** Fraser's own version (why-gap → "the best marketers ask WHY" → soft follow CTA `@frasercottrell`). No comment-keyword / no freebie.

**Metrics (19h):** Views 1,225 · Reached 824 · Avg watch **6s** · Follows **0** · Likes 4 · Comments 0 · Shares ~1 · Saves 0
- Skip **35.1% (Lower / good)** · Share 0.1% (Higher / good) · Like 0.5% (Lower) · Save 0.0% (typical) · Repost 0.0% (typical)

**Read:**
- ✅ **Hook works.** Low skip + 6s watch = the scroll-stop and hold are landing. The hardest part is working.
- ✅ Share rate above typical — the angle carries some social currency.
- ❌ **Conversion leaked:** 0 follows / 0 comments / 0 saves from 824 reached. They watched, didn't act.

**Likely causes (most-likely first):**
1. **Profile not set up** — cold viewers tap through to an unclear bio/no highlights → no follow. Follows convert *at the profile*. (Stranger test still unaddressed = biggest suspect.)
2. **No comment-keyword CTA** — soft "drop a follow" instead of "comment WHY for [freebie]". Nothing manufacturing comments or capturing leads.
3. **Abstract payoff** — caption teaches a principle but leaves nothing concrete to save.
4. **n=1** — single reel, not a pattern.

**Actions:**
- [ ] Set up bio + 1–2 highlights **before** next posts (highest leverage, one-time)
- [ ] Add comment-keyword CTA + build the "5 questions" WHY freebie
- [ ] Test a variant with the 3 questions listed in-caption (gives a save-reason)
- [ ] Post the rest of the battery, compare skip / watch / follows

**Early hypothesis (hold loosely):** the *agency call-out / mirror* hook style stops the scroll well. Keep testing it. The blocker on results is downstream of the hook — profile + CTA — not the hook itself.
