# Hooks Swipe File

From Georgia Socials (Fraser_Hooks.pdf). Use for trial reels, carousels, and main-feed reels.
Don't copy word-for-word — take the shape, fill with Fraser's real specifics. Specificity is what makes a hook land.

## Story / template hooks (fill the brackets)

1. "I spent [TIME] trying to fix [PROBLEM]… until I discovered [RESOLUTION]."
2. "I thought [BELIEF] was the reason I couldn't [DESIRED OUTCOME]… but I was wrong."
3. "The biggest mistake I made with [TOPIC] was [MISTAKE]… here's what finally worked."
4. "Nobody warned me that [PROBLEM] would happen when I started [THING]…"
5. "I went from [BEFORE STATE] to [AFTER STATE] after changing one thing: [KEY CHANGE]."
6. "For years, I struggled with [PROBLEM] until I realized [IMPORTANT LESSON]."
7. "I almost gave up on [GOAL] because of [OBSTACLE]… then I tried [RESOLUTION]."
8. "I wish I knew [LESSON] before I wasted [TIME/MONEY/ENERGY] on [MISTAKE]."
9. "Everyone told me [COMMON ADVICE], but doing the opposite helped me [RESULT]."

## Educational
- "The biggest mistake I see in almost every ad — and it's costing them [XYZ]…"
- "If your ads aren't converting, check this first."
- "Most brands are wasting money because of this."
- "The one thing every high-converting ad has in common."
- "Before you launch your next campaign, watch this."

## Contrarian
- "More ad spend isn't your problem."
- "Your creative matters more than your targeting."
- "Stop copying viral ads."
- "You're probably testing your ads the wrong way."
- "Great ads aren't creative for the sake of being creative."

## Client & agency insights
- "Here's what we changed to increase conversions."
- "The first thing we look at when auditing an ad account."
- "What clients think they need vs. what they actually need."
- "The reason this campaign finally worked."
- "Here's what we'd do differently if we started again."

## Ad breakdowns
- "This is why Apple's ads feel so different."
- "Here's why this Nike campaign worked."
- "Everyone's talking about this ad — but they missed the real reason it performed."
- "I'd give this ad a 4/10... here's why."
- "Let's break down why this campaign made millions."

## Agency call-outs (mirror the buyer's frustration with their current agency)
- "If your agency keeps blaming the algorithm every month..."
- "Your agency should be showing you this every week."
- "If your agency can't explain why an ad worked..."
- "Stop accepting 'we're testing' as an excuse."
- "If you're paying an agency and still guessing what's working..."

## Founders
- "Founders: stop approving ads based on what *you* like."
- "If you're the one giving the final sign-off on every advert..."
- "You're too close to your brand to judge your own ads objectively."
- "Founders, here's the mistake I see every week."
- "Your product isn't the problem — your messaging is."

## Ecommerce brands
- "Ecommerce brands keep making this expensive mistake."
- "If your store converts but your ads don't..."
- "You're driving traffic to an offer that isn't exciting anyone."
- "Most ecommerce brands test offers too slowly."
- "If you're relying on discounts to sell..."

---

**Best format (from Georgia):** Identity → Pain → Curiosity. Lead by naming *who* it's for, name the *pain*, then withhold the answer to create *curiosity*. Agency call-outs + founder + ecommerce hooks above are already identity-led — Fraser's strongest angles because they mirror the exact buyer.
