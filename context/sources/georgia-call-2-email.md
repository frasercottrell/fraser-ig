Great chatting earlier today Fraser!

We reviewed the content that's currently working as trial reels and identified 3 successful hook frameworks to build around:

1. Contrarian expertise: "The first thing I check in a broken ad account isn't targeting."
2. Competitive gap / FOMO: "Your competitor is testing 10x more ads than you. They're using AI."
3. Pattern recognition / authority: "The one thing every high-converting ad has in common."

The key takeaway is to stop trying to invent completely new hooks every time and instead build repeatable hook families, then test different angles within each.

**1. Create more variations of the 3 winning hook frameworks**
- "Most brands think X. Actually, Y."
- "The first thing I check/do isn't X…"
- "Your competitor is doing X. Here's why."
- "Every winning X I've seen does Y."
- "I analysed X and noticed Y."

**2. Focus on testing, not just posting**
For each content idea, identify what we're testing — hook, angle, format, or topic — so we can understand why something performs.

**3. Build a swipe file / idea bank**
Keep collecting interesting ads, brands, AI developments, Reddit conversations, trends and examples that can be turned into content.

**Content ideas to explore over next 2 weeks:**

AI / advertising
- Why your competitors can now test 10x more ads with AI
- Why you should be using Claude Code to write ad scripts
- How to use AI to copy any winning static ad
- How AI is changing creative testing
- Meta is about to change how AI-generated ads work
- "Most brands are wasting money on ads because of this"
- "I've tested 10,000+ ads. Here's what actually works"

Ad breakdowns / case studies
- Break down the XX brands ad and explain why it works
- The foot-shaving device: how the brand scaled by showing people using the product
- "I scaled this business by 70% by showing pictures of people feet"

- The first thing I do when I sit down with a brand founder is XYZ.
- "Here's what happened when I took this basic static ad and rebuilt it with AI"

Show the ad or evidence on screen to build more authority.

Emerging ad formats

- How AI slop / animated ads are changing creative
- "Yapper" Facebook ads, how yapping has changed advertising forever

Founder / expert content
- "The first thing I do when I sit down with a brand founder is…"
- What most founders get wrong about their ads
- How to turn a founder's story into an ad

You can also start jumping on Stories in real time to build up more content and eventually turn the best ones into Highlights:
1. Do regular Q&As: ask yourself questions about ads, creative, AI, scaling, hooks, etc., and answer them on camera. You can literally seed your own questions to get the conversation going.
2. Show real-time examples of successful brands: film your laptop/results, talk to camera and explain what the brand is doing, why you think it's working, and what other advertisers can learn from it.

Turn these 3 ideas into carousel posts, with the first 1–2 slides focused heavily on the hook, then use the rest of the carousel to tell the story, break down the insight, and build the argument.

For each carousel:
- Slide 1: Big pattern-interrupting hook
- Slide 2: Reinforce the hook / create curiosity to keep them swiping
- Slides 3–7: Tell the story, break down the insight and give real examples
- Final slide: Strong CTA "Comment NYX and I'll send you [relevant resource/next step]."

The 3 carousels:
1. Most brands are wasting money because XYZ — Use the carousel to reveal the mistake, show the way brands should be creating/testing ads, and give examples of what they should be doing instead.
2. I've tested 10,000+ ads. This is what actually works. — Use the credibility of the number to break down the patterns you've repeatedly seen across winning ads.
3. Bonus Carousel — Experiment with whatever story about any topic you want within your content pillars and we'll see how it performs!

Key objective: make the first two slides so strong that someone who sees Slide 1 & 2 feels like they need to swipe to find out the answer, then use the remaining slides to deliver enough value that the final CTA (comment for freebie) feels natural.
