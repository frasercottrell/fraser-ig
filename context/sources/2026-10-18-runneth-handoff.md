## Hard-won rules from Fraser's rejections (full text in the ledger MISS notes)
- On-screen hook's only job is stop + watch (Georgia's five questions). Never mention the freebie/list/download in the hook; that lives in the caption + keyword.
- Cold trial hook = viewer named + crowd proof (other brands, competitors, their feed, never Fraser's own stats) + unresolved gap + "ads" in the line (AI-character winner broke the "ads" rule).
- Don't loop on the same six facts (10,000 ads, 34 hooks, Grüns, feet 70%, competitor volume, agency one-hook).
- Build lines off his verbatim transcript sentences, not new lines with his tics sprinkled on. Run `fraser-ig/scripts/voice-lint.py`, no em dashes, no stacked fragments.
- Hand-over: hooks one line each, at most one sentence after. Talking-head idea = one on-screen line under 12 words, one body point, one-word CTA.
- Trial reels = variations on logged winners. Talking heads = topics not yet posted.
