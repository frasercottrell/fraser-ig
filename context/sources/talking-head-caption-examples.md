## Value listicle (the YAP one — closest model for Fraser's teach captions)

I am convinced everyone is faking their yapping and, as someone who loves to script but make it sound like a yap, there are things I can teach you.

Back in April, I had 4,500 followers. By the end of July, we hit 70,000. A huge part of that growth came from FAKING yaps.

Before I get into the 3 most important things, it's worth noting that Yap is a newer trend, coined by @bonusfootage and run with by @jessijeanhome, but it's not a new concept. It's simply a casual, conversational talking head.

After six years of running a social agency, I can promise you that talking ads are usually planned and scripted. Call it a yap if you like, but the successful ones are rarely off the cuff. There IS strategy behind them.

Three things I'd focus on:

1. Have natural intonation and energy. Talk with your hands, vary your voice and don't sound flat.
2. Script your content, BUT stop relying on a fucking teleprompter. We can tell. Learn it line by line and keep your delivery natural.
3. Add a credential line after your hook and use verbal, written and caption hooks to qualify why people should listen.

Bonus: tease what's coming next throughout the video. Your brain hates unsatisfied curiosity.

If you want to see all of this in action, watch the video again. I've been doing it the entire way through.
