# Content Pillars

Four pillars. Percentages = rough share of content. Every pillar serves the same goal:
**"If anyone understands ad creative, it's Fraser."**

---

## 1. Why Ads Work — 40%

The authority pillar. Break down the **psychology** behind high-performing (and failing) campaigns.

**Key move:** review **well-known brands** — the recognition earns the scroll-stop in the first 5 seconds because people already have context/opinions.
> Nike · Apple · Airbnb · Gymshark · Liquid Death · Duolingo

**Content ideas / hooks:**
- Why this Nike ad went viral
- The psychology behind Apple's marketing
- Why this Meta ad generated millions
- The biggest mistake brands make with creative
- Why this ad failed (negative hooks work)

Goal: saveable + shareable + educational.

---

## 2. Inside the Ad World — 25%

Pull back the curtain. Insider agency access people can't get elsewhere. Also humanises Fraser and shows what working with the agency is like.

**Content ideas:**
- How agencies brainstorm campaigns
- How clients approve creatives
- What happens before an ad goes live (vlog / real-time style)
- Creative testing strategies
- Campaign planning
- Behind the scenes of shoots
- Industry trends and hot takes
- **The AI angle** (fits here): latest AI tools + product releases; how agencies use AI in creative workflows; which AI tools are worth it vs hype; how AI is changing advertising
  - Note: AI content performs but can attract the *wrong* audience (quick-buck/AI-hype crowd). Frame it for operators, not tourists.

---

## 3. Client Wins & Case Studies — 20%

Credibility through proof + storytelling. **Lead the hook with a stat**, then explain *how* it was achieved (the lesson), not just the outcome. Teaching while selling.

**Stat-led hooks:**
- "How we increased ROAS by 217%"
- "This ad generated $1.2M in revenue"
- "The creative that doubled conversions"
- "Why this campaign achieved a 6.4x ROAS"
- "How one creative reduced CPA by 43%"

**Content ideas:**
- Before vs after creative
- Why this campaign increased ROAS
- Lessons from a successful launch
- Creative iterations
- Client transformations (problem → what we did → result + lesson)

Best pillar for sales conversions.

---

## 4. Founder Perspective — 15%

Trust + connection. The journey of running an agency, industry opinions, lessons learned — always **through the lens of building authority.** Personal content *supports* expertise, never replaces it.

**Content ideas:**
- Running an ad agency (real lessons)
- Lessons from client meetings
- Building a creative business
- Opinions / hot takes on the industry
- Humour welcome — keep it real and natural

Rule: people follow for the expertise first; the personal stuff deepens the relationship *after* they see you're an expert.

---

## Hook formula (all pillars)

**Identity → Pain → Curiosity**

Examples:
- "DTC founders spending £50k+ a month on Meta… you're probably making this creative mistake."
- "If your brand relies on paid ads, watch this before your next campaign."
- "Marketing managers: here's why your best-performing ad suddenly stopped working."
- "If you're scaling an e-commerce brand, don't ignore this."

**Mirror hooks:** mirror the viewer's exact experience back at them so they think "how do you know that?" The more specific, the more it triggers.
