# Instagram Strategy — Fraser Cottrell

## The goal

Make Fraser the **go-to voice of authority in the ad creative space.**

Every piece of content reinforces that positioning and answers one question:

> **"What can I teach my audience today?"**

The test: someone lands on the profile and thinks —
> *"If anyone understands ad creative, it's Fraser."*

## The shift

De-emphasise office/studio updates and behind-the-scenes filler.
Emphasise content that is:

- **Educational** — teaching practical marketing and creative principles.
- **Saveable** — frameworks, checklists, breakdowns, insights people come back to.
- **Shareable** — stuff marketers and founders send to their teams.
- **Conversion-focused** — building trust and demonstrating expertise so people naturally want to join the course or work with the agency.

## What the content should become known for

1. Breaking down **why ads work**.
2. Teaching **creative strategy**.
3. Sharing **insider agency knowledge**.
4. Demonstrating **real client results**.
5. Giving **practical advice people can apply immediately**.

## Every post checklist

- Does it teach something real?
- Is it worth saving?
- Would a marketer send it to their team?
- Does it reinforce "Fraser = ad creative authority"?
- Is it in Fraser's voice? (see tone-of-voice.md)
